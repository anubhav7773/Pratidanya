## 2. Bar Council of India (BCI) Rule 36 Declaration
1. **Zero Solicitation / Zero Advertisement:** The application does not publish advocate contact details to the general public, does not maintain a directory for litigant discovery, and operates solely as a private, chamber-restricted legal research assistant for licensed advocates enrolled under Section 30 of the Advocates Act, 1961.
2. **Professional Verification Gate:** Under Section 35 of the Advocates Act, 1961, the platform mandates that every precedent, statutory ground, and factual analysis generated by AI must be individually reviewed, verified, and certified by the advocate prior to export or printing.
