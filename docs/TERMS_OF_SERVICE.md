## 2. Section 35 Gate: Mandatory Human Verification of AI Outputs
1. **AI Output Is Subordinate Research:** Precedent summaries, draft bail grounds, and statutory comparative notes generated by प्रतिज्ञा are automated research aids and do **NOT** constitute legal advice, judicial certification, or binding authority.
2. **Advocate Responsibility:** Under **Section 35 of the Advocates Act, 1961**, the advocate assumes sole, non-delegable professional responsibility for verifying all case facts, citations, procedural timelines, and statutory provisions before signing, filing, or tendering any pleading before a court of law.
3. **Verification Gate:** The application enforces a mandatory statutory declaration checkbox requiring the advocate to manually review each citation before petition PDF compilation.

## 3. Bar Council of India (BCI) Rule 36 Compliance
1. **Zero Public Advertising:** प्रतिज्ञा does not publish advocate listings, ratings, testimonials, or fee quotes to the general public.
2. **Chamber Isolation:** No advocate's caseload, client identities, or pleading drafts are visible to any other subscriber on the platform.
